Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
App: Midlia: Menopause Tracker (iOS)
Developer and data controller: Vladislava Bogaturova, Poland (Romana Ciesielskiego 4b/118, 31-587 Kraków)
Contact: hello@midlia.app

  • Everything you enter into Midlia — symptoms, hot flashes, cycle, medications, notes, daily factors — is stored only on your device. We never receive it and never see it.
  • Midlia has no account and no server.
  • The app contains no analytics and no crash-reporting service. We collect no usage statistics at all.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your data for advertising.

1. What stays on your device

The following is created by you and stored locally on your phone:

  • symptoms and their severity, hot flash times, and the daily factors you log (sleep, alcohol, caffeine, exercise, stress, water, fasting);
  • period (cycle) entries, if you turn cycle logging on;
  • medications and supplements you add, with doses and reminder times;
  • notes, your onboarding answers (for example the symptoms you chose and how long you've noticed changes) and your app settings.

This information is health data. We do not have access to it. It is not uploaded, synced or backed up by us.

Because the data lives only on your device:

  • if you delete the app, you lose the data, unless you exported a backup;
  • your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup) may include the app's data, depending on your phone settings. That is controlled by your device settings and by Apple, not by us;
  • reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your phone. They are not sent from a server.

2. Apple Health

If you allow it, Midlia reads Sleep Analysis from Apple Health so you don't have to type your hours of sleep by hand. Midlia reads only that category, never writes anything to Health, and the data stays on your device like the rest of your diary. You can withdraw access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Midlia. We do not use health data for advertising and never disclose it to third parties.

3. What you can choose to share

  • Doctor report and exports. When you create a PDF report or export your data as CSV or JSON, the file is created on your device. You decide whether and where to share it (email, messages, cloud storage, print). Once you share a file, it is governed by the privacy practices of the service you send it to, not by this policy.
  • Support email. If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message, and optionally the app version, language and operating system. We use it to reply and to improve the app. Please don't include health details you don't want us to see.

4. Information collected automatically

None by us. This version of Midlia contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no advertising SDK. It does not collect usage statistics, does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not create any identifier that reaches us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple (App Store). We never receive your card or account payment details. To check whether your purchase is still valid, the app sends your App Store receipt to Apple's own verification service and receives back the product identifier and the expiry date. The receipt goes to Apple only; it contains no diary data, and no third party is involved.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not use data for advertising, profiling, tracking across other companies' apps or websites, or data brokerage.
  • We do not use your health entries to train any model.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

  • Your diary (health data): we do not receive or process it; it is stored by you on your device. Any processing by the app on your phone is carried out for you, at your direction.
  • Apple Health data: your explicit consent, given in the iOS permission dialog and withdrawable at any time.
  • Purchases: performance of our contract with you (providing the Pro features you bought) and our legal obligations.
  • Support emails: our legitimate interest in answering you.

7. Retention

  • Diary data: on your device until you delete it or the app.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple as long as needed to operate your subscription and for legal and accounting reasons.
  • Support emails: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, unless we need to keep them longer by law.

8. International transfers

We operate no servers and transfer no personal data ourselves. Apple processes purchase data under its own privacy policy and may process it outside your country, including in the United States.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR and similar laws (including CCPA/CPRA in California) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, and receive a copy of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

What this means in Midlia. We hold no copy of your diary, so there is nothing to request from us in that regard. You control it directly:

Right How you use it in Midlia
Access / copy of your data Settings → Your data → Export data (CSV or JSON).
Correct Edit any entry on the Calendar or Today screen.
Delete Settings → Your data → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes it.
Withdraw consent for Apple Health iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Midlia.
Portability Export in JSON or CSV (see above).

The only personal data we may hold is the content of emails you sent us. To ask about or delete it, write to hello@midlia.app with the subject "Privacy request". We will reply within 30 days. For purchase records, contact Apple. If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Midlia is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your diary is stored in your device's app storage, protected by the operating system's sandboxing and, if you've set one, your device passcode and encryption. No security is perfect. Please keep your device locked, and treat exported files and PDFs as sensitive.

12. California and other U.S. state notices

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in U.S. state privacy laws. We do not collect or use sensitive personal information (including health information) to infer characteristics about you, because we do not receive your diary.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app before the change takes effect. If a future version of the app adds analytics or crash reporting, this page will say so before that version ships.
