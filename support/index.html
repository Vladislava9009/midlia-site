Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Midlia has no account and no server, so your diary never leaves the device unless you export or share it yourself. See the Privacy Policy.

I changed phones. How do I keep my diary?

Settings → Your data → Export data, then import the file on the new phone. A restored iPhone backup usually brings the diary across too, but an export is the reliable way.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid, but Pro isn't showing

Open the paywall or Settings → Midlia Pro and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the Apple ID you bought with. If it still doesn't appear, email us and mention which plan you bought and when.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders don't arrive

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Midlia, and make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself, so they also work offline.

Can Midlia tell me what's wrong with me?

No. Midlia is a diary and statistics tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose and doesn't suggest treatments. The patterns it shows describe your own entries, and the doctor report is a summary to bring to an appointment, not a medical record.
